      What this model does not claim

      • It is sculpted procedurally from geometric primitives, not derived from a patient's CT or MRI. Chamber shapes, wall thickness, valves and vessel courses are simplified.
      • The heart is posed didactically, with the ventricular septum roughly edge-on to the section plane so both ventricles sit side by side, as in textbook drawings. In the chest the RV lies more anteriorly and the apex points leftward.
      • Particles show flow direction and timing only. They are not CFD, Doppler velocities or measured shunt fractions.
      • The transannular-patch scenario shows a late, significant PR example with RV dilation, not what every patch produces.
      • The conduit scenario shows a different complex pathway (TOF with pulmonary atresia), not routine repair of classic TOF.
      • The wall-thickness readout compares the model's own walls. It is not a measurement, dose, risk estimate or treatment recommendation.
