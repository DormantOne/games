About this model — what's real, what's a toy
This is a genuine transformer forward pass, written out small enough to watch. It is not a trained language model and its answers carry no authority — it exists to make the machinery legible.

The attention and feed-forward weights are randomly initialized and frozen, so you are watching the mechanism, not learned skill — an untrained attention head routes more or less arbitrarily, which is itself the point of the Learn tab. The output layer was trained by gradient descent on a dozen example sentences (the same algorithm shown in Learn), which is why the next-token guesses land on-theme instead of random. Real models run this same arithmetic with billions of weights, dozens of blocks, thousands of hidden dimensions, and a trained tokenizer.
