Protoneuron Pendulum — Fair Search A/B

Champion-style search against fitness-map basin search, on your lab's own pendulum, brain, mutation operator and fitness (copied unchanged from the Triple-Pendulum Lab v12). Four arms, so each difference can be told apart:
  • A0, champions as written: the original baseline. Its hall of fame rejects any brain within 4 % wiring distance of a member, and a 3-mutation child is at most 0.26 % away, so it rarely changes and 55 % of parents stay the starting brain.
  • A1, champions with the hall of fame fixed: an improved near-copy replaces its weaker neighbour instead of being rejected. Everything else as A0.
  • A2, A1 with the map's step sizes: 2, 3 or 5 mutations (55 / 35 / 10 %), like the map search's local samples. This separates "smaller steps" from "searching several peaks".
  • B, fitness-map basin search: as written, minus one redundant re-evaluation of an already scored brain.
Fair by construction: for each seed every arm starts from the same newborn brain, trains on the same 12 cases, is tested on the same unseen holdout cases, and spends exactly the same number of evaluations (1 + generations × samples, counted and shown). A0, A1 and A2 share their random-number stream. Results are compared seed by seed with an exact paired permutation test.

Settings

Workers 0 = all cores but one. Each (arm, seed) is one job; jobs run in parallel.
