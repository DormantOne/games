Slit Lamp

Four ophthalmic patients. You commit to a diagnosis, order tests and treatment, and the eye responds to what you do — including the wrong things.

The controls aren't obvious, so there's a practice patient who talks you through them. It blinks whichever knob you need next and says out loud what it does. Two minutes.

Rather read it? Written instructions. Both stay available from ? How this works in the header.

  1. Pick a caseThe three tabs at the top right. Each one resets to a fresh presentation.
  2. Read the presentationRight-hand column: the story, then the examination findings. Everything you need to make the call is in there.
  3. Commit a differential — this unlocks treatmentDrag the sliders until they total 100, then press Commit. Until you do, the treatment and procedure orders stay greyed out; only examination and bedside orders are live. If nothing seems clickable, this is why. Leaving weight on undifferentiated is a legitimate answer.
  4. Place orders and watch the eyeClick any order to place it. Each one spends time on the clock and changes the patient — pupil, corneal clarity, pressure, retina. Harmful orders are deliberately clickable, and the log tells you what you did. Some are gated: order a laser through a hazy cornea and you'll get a Blocked line explaining what to fix first.
  5. Conclude the caseThe Conclude case button in Debrief scores critical actions, harms, calibration and time-to-treatment, then gives the teaching points. Export session saves the whole run as JSON; Restart case wipes it.
The controls around the eye don't change the patient. The three view tabs (external, cross-section, fundus) and the two toggles beneath it are ways of looking: fluorescein-plus-cobalt and the slit beam are examination manoeuvres, so switching them on and off is free and costs no time.
Two guided runs. Case 00 · Practice teaches the interface with voice and blinking controls. Case 01 has a walkthrough that narrates the medicine and places the orders for you, pausing for you to click Continue. Both start from the ▶ button in the header.
