Protoneuron Triple-Pendulum Connectome Lab v12

A deliberately undifferentiated recurrent population controls a three-link inverted pendulum. Every protoneuron has exactly 3 incoming slots; synaptic weights and communicated activations are ternary −1, 0, +1. This build runs one method only — evolution with champions — and adds a diversity archive so the champions you keep are behaviourally different brains rather than eight copies of the same one. The survival lane, episode length, and every champion-feed ratio are editable while it runs.
