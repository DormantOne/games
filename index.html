Perceptual learning · molecular eye

Learn to see chemistry.

An organic chemist can glance at a cluster of atoms and know caffeine from theobromine. That's not cleverness — it's a trained eye, and you can build one too.

Count a look-alike target in a slowly tumbling, foggy ball of soft overlapping spheres. When time runs out it just blanks — answer at your own pace. Drag to turn it, go fullscreen, hit Auto-play to watch, and reach the top to unlock the reaction puzzle.
