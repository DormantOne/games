Teaching model · adjust one force at a time, then add a second process.

Model: pH = 6.1 + log₁₀(HCO₃⁻ / (0.03 × PaCO₂)). Arrow contributions are calculated as changes in log pH relative to a 24 mEq/L bicarbonate and 40 mmHg CO₂ baseline, then displayed as a force-field metaphor. Compensation follows simplified rules (Winter’s formula for metabolic acidosis; approximate expected CO₂ for metabolic alkalosis; acute-to-chronic bicarbonate response to respiratory changes). This is not a validated clinical calculator, kinetic simulator, or substitute for measured blood gases and clinical judgment.
