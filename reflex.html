    What is accurate here, and what is a model. The anatomy, segmental levels, peripheral nerves, conduction velocities, latencies, transmitters, receptors, channels and clinical patterns are drawn from standard neurology and neurophysiology. Cell positions inside the cord outline are schematic rather than a laminar map. The reflex gain is a deliberately simple lumped model: afferent integrity, synaptic efficacy, motoneuron pool size, five inhibitory terms and an intrinsic-excitability term feed one equation whose output is mapped onto the 0–4+ scale. It reproduces the textbook patterns and responds sensibly when you lesion a pathway, but it is not a biophysical simulation of a motoneuron pool, and grades in real patients vary widely between normal people.
