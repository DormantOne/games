Architecture

undifferentiated N neurons exactly 3 incoming / neuron arbitrary recurrent edges synchronous updates weights ∈ {−1,0,+1} activation ∈ {−1,0,+1}
There are exactly 3N neuron→neuron connection slots. The connectome cannot get denser without violating the 3-incoming constraint; optimization therefore means finding a more useful arrangement of those fixed slots, not adding more edges.

What is optimized?

Training has three modes. RL is reward-only policy search: it mutates ternary weights and sparse connections, evaluates the actual controller, and keeps improvements. Teacher uses direct supervised control. Hybrid combines teacher guidance with exploration while fading the teacher out over 80 episodes. Connectome optimization is separate: it proposes a random replacement for one incoming source, evaluates the resulting controller, and keeps the change only when the measured evaluation improves. Every accepted graph still has exactly 3 incoming edges per neuron.
