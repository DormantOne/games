The brain

Every cell is a leaky ODE whose time constant is gated by its own input, so it can be sluggish one moment and fast the next. There is no clock input and no phase input — if a rhythm appears, it came from the network's own evolved time constants. Each row is scaled to its own range. A period is only reported once a cell's signal has decorrelated and come back — a cell that merely drifts gets a dash, because smooth is not rhythmic.

Champion pools

Admission is half how good a dog is and half how differently it moves — a quarter and three quarters in strange. Fossils are frozen at the end of each cycle and can never be evicted, however thoroughly they are later beaten, because the thing that beat them may turn out to be a dead end.

Behaviour space

Difference is measured in what a dog does, not in its genes. Two genomes can differ in 200 places and produce the same gait, and two near-identical genomes can produce a walk and a hop — so the pools compare six numbers describing cadence, duty factor, alternation, ride height, speed and gentleness. This is two of those six at a time. When the cloud shrinks to a knot, that is a collapse, and two pools start lowering their standards until something genuinely different gets in.

Does it work from a cold start?

One run from one pose is one sample. This re-runs every champion from twelve fresh starting poses, which is the number that tells you whether you have a gait or a lucky launch.

One file, no install: the physics, the network, the evolution and this page are all in it, and it runs from a local copy with no server. Everything the dog is told about what "good" means is in the three gate objects near the top of the engine; the pools and the cycling curriculum follow it.

A planar dog is much easier than a three-dimensional one: no roll, no yaw, no lateral balance. Position-based dynamics is stable and fast but not torque-accurate, and the muscles are compliant distance constraints rather than motors with real limits. Nothing here transfers to hardware. The ground is flat and uniform, and one run is one sample — promotion times vary a lot with the seed.
