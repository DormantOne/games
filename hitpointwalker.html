Does a health budget change the outcome? Two populations from the same seed evolve in parallel. The HP arm carries a health pool that injuries (impact, joint torque, instability, falls) drain; at 0 it dies, and its fitness is distance × survival-fraction — it must travel far and stay alive. The distance arm is the plain control: forward distance over a fixed life, no health. The running graph pits their champion distance against each other every generation; the two viewports show both champions from a shared camera (drag either to orbit both, so you compare gaits at the same angle; top-down reveals drift). Biopsy reports whether they tie on raw distance (the honest ceiling) and whether the HP arm buys extra lifespan for the same ground — a longer-lived animal covering the same distance more sustainably.
