Educational decision explorer — not a clinical decision-support or treatment-ordering system. Assumes a newly diagnosed, potentially curable, nonmetastatic case and uses illustrative pathways. True TNM stage, grade, margins, genomic assay eligibility, pathologic response, comorbidities, pregnancy, patient preferences and local protocols can change management. Durations are approximate and can overlap. HER2+ here means IHC 3+ or IHC 2+ with ISH amplification, not simply “HER2-low.”
