Technical thoughts

What this model can—and cannot—teach.

This is deliberately a compact evolutionary mechanics sandbox, not a validated biomechanical model. Its value is in making the curriculum, controller ablation and transfer dynamics legible in one file.

01 · Mechanics

Constraint-based body

Four particles form three fixed-length segments; iterative distance constraints keep the rods rigid and ground projection supplies contact and friction. Joint pulses are applied along the joint angle's gradient, so they are internal torques: they bend the body but cannot push its centre of mass. v1 rotated the outer particles about a pinned middle one, which leaked momentum on every pulse—evolution learned to fly.

02 · Controller

Pulse, not continuous torque

The neural genome maps phase, tilt, joint state, vertical velocity, contact and BX impact context into two gated joint targets. The LUT alternative bins phase, lean and contact. The gate windows are fixed, so both joints are passive for about a third of every phase cycle—quiet standing on a point foot is hard, and hopping in place is a common answer.

03 · BX biopsy

A causal ablation

BX is contact multiplied by downward centre-of-mass speed, clipped to a unit impact signal. Each card re-scores its champion with BX zeroed (ablate Δ); "Evolve without BX" instead trains a whole run without it. Contact and vertical speed are still separate inputs, so a small Δ does not prove impact context is unused, and the LUT controller never sees BX.

04 · Curriculum

Transfer is useful, not free

With forward migration enabled, elites flow only Standing → Bouncing → Bouncing forward and still compete under the destination objective. The biopsy reports each champion's migrant ancestry, so a destination champion that is just an imported clone shows up as one.

05 · Fitness

Multi-term objectives need care

Standing credits height only up to upright posture (1.17 m), so flight earns nothing there. Bounce adds vertical range, airborne fraction and touchdowns—a touchdown counts only after three airborne steps, so contact chatter can't be farmed. Forward adds signed displacement. Scores are comparable within a column, not across columns.

06 · Limitations

Where to go next

Each joint's motor is now capped like the MuJoCo walker's (0.7 × body weight × segment length by default; its motors are 0.6–0.8 on that scale). Unlimited is v2's joint, which reached any bend under any load and let bodies jump metres. Rigid-body impulses, randomized starts and replicated seeds still come before attributing performance to BX or migration.

v2.1 · Joint strength is part of the run's settings and the biopsy; Unlimited reproduces v2 bit for bit.
